⛏ A2Launcher — Play Minecraft in Browser Online

Free Minecraft web client by Ayush Rajdev and Anzar Iqbal. Play Minecraft directly in your browser without downloading or installing anything. Works on any device with a modern browser.

No Java needed • No download required • 100% free & open source

🎮 How to Play Minecraft in Browser
A2Launcher lets you play Minecraft in your browser using Eaglercraft technology. It's the easiest way to play Minecraft online without installing Java or downloading any files. Simply choose a server from the list below and click to join — the game loads instantly in your browser window.

💡 About A2Launcher Minecraft Web Client

A2Launcher is a free Minecraft web client built by Ayush Rajdev and Anzar Iqbal from Obedullaganj, Madhya Pradesh, India.

It uses Eaglercraft technology to run Minecraft in your browser using WebGL and WebSocket. You can play Minecraft online on any device — PC, laptop, tablet, or phone — without installing Java or downloading any files.

Popular Minecraft servers you can join:
• Eaglercraft Official — wss://eaglercraft.com:25569
• Eaglercraft Network — wss://play.eaglercraft.net:25569
• MC Land — wss://mc.lc:25569
• Build The Earth — wss://server.buildtheearth.net:25569
• ShieldPVP — wss://eagler.shieldpvp.com:25569

A2Launcher is the best alternative to MessCraft for playing Minecraft in your browser. It's completely open source, ad-free, and maintained by the community.
